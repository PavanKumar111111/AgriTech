Weather refers to the atmospheric conditions in a specific place over a short period, usually from minutes to weeks. It includes elements such as temperature, humidity, wind speed, air pressure, and precipitation. Unlike climate, which describes long-term weather patterns, weather is highly dynamic and can change rapidly

Elements of Weather :

1. Temperature

Temperature is the measure of heat energy in the atmosphere. It varies depending on latitude, altitude, time of day, and season. The Sun plays a significant role in heating the Earth's surface, which in turn warms the air above it. Temperature affects daily activities, agriculture, and even human health.

2. Humidity

Humidity refers to the amount of water vapor in the air. High humidity makes the air feel warmer, while low humidity results in dry conditions. It plays a crucial role in weather phenomena such as fog, dew, and precipitation.

3. Air Pressure

Air pressure is the force exerted by the weight of air molecules. It is measured using a barometer and is usually given in millibars (mb) or inches of mercury (inHg). High-pressure areas typically bring clear, stable weather, while low-pressure systems lead to clouds, storms, and precipitation.

4. Wind

Wind is the movement of air caused by differences in air pressure. It varies in speed and direction and significantly impacts weather patterns. Winds transport heat and moisture, influencing weather conditions over large areas.

5. Precipitation

Precipitation occurs when water droplets or ice crystals in clouds grow large enough to fall to the ground. Forms of precipitation include:

  • Rain - Liquid water droplets falling from clouds.
  • Snow - Ice crystals forming when temperatures are below freezing.
  • Sleet - Frozen raindrops that reach the ground as ice pellets.
  • Hail - Hard ice balls formed in thunderstorm clouds.
